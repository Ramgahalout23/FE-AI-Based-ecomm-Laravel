🎯 4 Platforms — Kaun Si Ad Kahan Chalegi?

Har platform ka apna audience, apna format, aur apna purpose hai. Neeche samjhte hain:

VISUAL
📸

Instagram Ads

Best for: Lifestyle, Fashion, Visual Products
Kya hota hai? Photo/Video feed mein, Stories mein, ya Reels ke beech mein dikhta hai.
Kiske liye? 18-34 age, women-heavy, fashion & beauty ke liye best.
Format: Square/vertical images ya 15-30sec videos
TARGETED
👍

Facebook Ads

Best for: Broad Targeting, Retargeting, Sales
Kya hota hai? News Feed, Marketplace, ya Right Column mein banner dikhta hai.
Kiske liye? 25-55 age, dono genders, specific interests target kar sakte ho.
Format: Image, Carousel (multiple images), Video, ya Slideshow
INTENT
▶️

Google / YouTube Ads

Best for: Search Intent, Video Marketing
Kya hota hai? Google Search pe top par dikhta hai, ya YouTube video se pehle/middle mein.
Kiske liye? Jo log already search kar rahe hain — high intent buyers.
Format: Text (search), Image (display), Video (YouTube)
DIRECT
💬

WhatsApp Ads

Best for: Direct Broadcasts, Personal Outreach
Kya hota hai? Subscribers ko direct broadcast bhejna, ya WhatsApp Business pe ad laga kar baat shuru karna.
Kiske liye? Existing customers, repeat buyers, local audience.
Format: Message template (text + image + button)

🤔 Kya Iske Liye Designed Templates Chahiye?
✅
Nahi, koi design ki zaroorat nahi!

Brand Ads tab khud hi presets provide karta hai. Aap bas click karo — budget ₹5000 auto-set ho jayega, platform select ho jayega, aur form fill ho jayega. Phir aap sirf creative URL (image/video link) dalo aur campaign ready!

🎨
Lekin creative (image/video) to dalni padegi

Preset campaign ki settings fill karta hai — name, platform, budget, objective. Lekin aapko apni product ki photo ya video link deni hogi jo ad mein dikhegi. Woh aap apne product catalog se le sakte ho ya upload kar sakte ho.

🤖 AI Tools Tab — ChatGPT Se Ad Copy Generate Karna
⚡ Ye sabse powerful feature hai!

AI Tools tab mein aap ChatGPT (OpenAI) ka use karke professional ad copy bana sakte ho — headlines, descriptions, CTAs, hashtags sab kuch. Pehle Settings → AI mein API key daalni hogi.

✨
Generate Copy
Headline + text + CTA + hashtags — ek click mein poori ad copy ready
🔀
A/B Variants
4 different angles (urgency, social proof, value, emotional) — test karo konsa better hai
🏆
Full Strategy
Campaign name + copy + budget + audience + KPIs — puri strategy ek saath
👥
Audience
Age, gender, interests, locations, best time — ChatGPT suggest karega target audience
🛠️ AI Tools Use Karne Ka Tarika (Step-by-Step)
  1. Settings → AI mein apna OpenAI API key daalo aur save karo
  2. Ads → AI Tools tab mein aao
  3. Optional Product select karo (taaki AI ko context mile)
  4. Platform choose karo (Instagram/Facebook/Google/WhatsApp) aur Tone select karo
  5. Koi bhi button click karo — Generate Copy, A/B Variants, Full Strategy, ya Audience
  6. AI ka result aayega — "Create Campaign" click karke seedha campaign form mein daal sakte ho!
📋 Complete Workflow — Ek Campaign Kaise Banega?
1

Settings mein OpenAI API Key daalo Must-Do

Kya karna hai: Admin → Settings → AI tab → API key paste karo → Save karo

💡 key nahi hai? platform.openai.com se free trial key le lo
2

Brand Tab se Preset chuno (ya direct Campaign banao)

Kya karna hai: Ads → Brand Ads → koi preset click karo. Form automatically fill ho jayega. Sirf product ki photo/video dalni hai.

🎯 Ya phir "New" button se khud bhi bana sakte ho
3

AI Tools se copy generate karo (optional but recommended)

Kya karta hai: AI Tools tab mein jaake product select karo, platform/tone choose karo, aur "Generate Copy" click karo. AI likh dega headline, description, CTA.

🤖 Phir "Create Campaign" button se seedha form mein bhar sakte ho
4

Campaign fill karo aur Save karo

Kya bharna hai: Name, Platform, Objective, Budget (₹), Creative URL (product photo/video link), Landing URL, Start/End date

📸 Creative URL = aapke product ki photo ya video ka link
5

Platform pe Push karo 🚀

Kya hota hai: Campaign table mein Upload button hai — usse click karo aur ad actual Instagram/Facebook/Google pe chala jayega.

⚠️ Push karne ke liye pehle Settings mein Meta ya Google ki credentials dalni hogi
6

Analytics Check karo 📊

Kya dekhte hain: Impressions, Clicks, CTR, Spent, ROAS — Analytics tab mein performance report dekho. Cross-platform comparison bhi available hai.

📈 Purane campaigns ka data bhi dekh sakte ho — "Load Advanced Performance Report" click karo
❓ Common Questions (Jo Aap Puchh Rahe Honge)
Q: Kya Brand Ads ke liye alag se designed templates/creatives banane padenge?

Nahi. Brand Ads tab mein 6 ready-made presets hain jo sirf click karne se campaign form fill kar dete hain. Aapko sirf apni product ki photo ya video ka link dena hai. Baaki sab (name, platform, budget, objective) auto-set ho jata hai.

Q: AI Tools ke liye kya OpenAI API key zaroori hai?

Haan. Settings → AI mein API key daalni padegi. OpenAI se free trial key le sakte ho. Key daalne ke baad server restart karna hoga.

Q: Instagram/Facebook pe actual ad push kaise karein?

Campaign table mein har campaign ke aage Upload aur Sync buttons hain. Upload click karo, ad platform pe push ho jayega. Sync click karo to live stats aajayenge. Lekin pehle Settings mein Meta access token aur ad account ID dalni hogi.

Q: Kaunsa platform choose karein?

Instagram = Fashion, lifestyle, visual products (young audience)
Facebook = Broad targeting, all products (older audience bhi)
Google/YouTube = High-intent buyers (jo search kar rahe hain)
WhatsApp = Direct broadcast to existing subscribers
Best strategy: Ek hi campaign multiple platforms pe banao aur phir Analytics mein compare karo!

Q: Product linking ka kya matlab hai?

Campaign table mein Link2 button hai. Usko click karo, product search karo, link karo. Linked products se AI automatically ad creative generate kar sakta hai (product ki photo + description use karke).

⚙️ Settings Ki Checklist — Pehle Ye Set Karo

Sabse pehle Settings → AI mein OpenAI key dalo. Baaki credentials optional hain jab tak actual platform pe push nahi kar rahe.
